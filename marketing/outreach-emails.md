# Outreach Emails

Backlink, listing, and partnership emails for five MTG sites. Each is short, specific, names the recipient's site by what it actually does, proposes one concrete thing, and offers a reciprocal link. No "leveraging synergies" language.

**Sending notes:**

- Send as the creator, from `moatsmjl@gmail.com`. Sign with first name only.
- Personalize the first sentence to the most recent thing the recipient site has shipped if you can spot it (their newsletter, a recent feature, a tweet).
- Stagger sends across a week. Never blast all five on the same day.
- Follow up once after 7 days if no reply. After that, drop it.
